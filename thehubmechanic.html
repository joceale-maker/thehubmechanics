FAQ

Common questions

Anything not answered here? Just ask during the free audit call.

Do I need to be on a paid HubSpot plan? ▾
Some features (like complex workflows, sequences, and reporting) require a paid HubSpot subscription. If you're on the free plan, we'll tell you honestly what's possible and what would require an upgrade — there's no pressure either way. Many quick fixes and setup improvements can be done on any plan.
How do you work with international clients? ▾
Fully remote. We use Zoom or Google Meet for calls, Loom for async walkthroughs, and email for updates. I'm based in the Philippines (GMT+8) but can flex around your timezone for calls — most European, AU, and US clients haven't had any issues scheduling.
How long does a project take? ▾
Quick Fix projects are typically completed within 3–5 business days. Full Optimization projects usually take 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. For retainer work, priority turnaround is within 1 business day. We'll give you an honest timeline before any work starts.
Will you need admin access to my HubSpot? ▾
Yes — to build and implement changes, we need to be added as a user with the right permissions. You can set this up as a limited admin role, and remove access any time after the project is complete. We document every change we make so you have full visibility.
What if I need something that's not in your packages? ▾
Just ask. The packages cover the most common needs, but plenty of work is scoped as custom projects. The free audit call is the best place to explain what you need — and we'll give you a straight quote for it with no pressure to buy.
Are you a certified HubSpot Partner? ▾
Not yet — partner certification is in progress. In the meantime, all work is backed by 3+ years of hands-on HubSpot experience in a real business environment, not just certifications. The lower prices are a direct benefit of not having partner agency overhead — and that changes nothing about the quality of the work.
